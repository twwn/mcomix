# Images the wiki pages show

SourceForge keeps an image as an attachment of the page that shows it, not
in the page's text, so these files are kept here. Attach each to its page
under exactly this name, in place of the old attachment, and no page needs
to change:

File | Attached to
-----|------------
`mcomix-mainwindow.png` | Documentation
`mcomix-library.png` | Documentation
`mcomix-external-commands.png` | External_Commands

They show MComix 4.0.0 under GTK 4, reduced to 256 colours. Moved to
GitHub, they go into `images/` beside the converted pages, as
`wiki/Readme.md` describes.

The pages in `mcomix-mainwindow.png`, and one of the covers in
`mcomix-library.png`, are from "The Potion of Flight", episode 1 of
[Pepper&Carrot](https://www.peppercarrot.com/) by David Revoy, licensed
under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and scaled
down; the book is `test/files/pepper-and-carrot`. The Documentation page
credits it under the first of them. Wherever one of these images is shown
on its own, as a project screenshot or in the AppStream metainfo, give the
same credit with it.
