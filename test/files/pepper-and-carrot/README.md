# Pepper&Carrot, episode 1: The Potion of Flight

`Pepper-and-Carrot_E01_The-Potion-of-Flight.cbz` holds the English title
strip and three pages of episode 1 of Pepper&Carrot, a webcomic by David
Revoy, published in May 2014 at <https://www.peppercarrot.com>.

- Art and story: David Revoy.
- English proofreading: Amireeti, Alex Gryson.
- Source: <https://www.peppercarrot.com/en/webcomic/ep01_Potion-of-Flight.html>,
  the low-resolution pages of the episode's sources.
- Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
  (CC BY 4.0), as <https://www.peppercarrot.com/en/license/index.html> states.

Changes made for MComix: the pages were scaled from 1200 to 800 pixels
wide and saved again as JPEG, the credits strip was left out, and a
`ComicInfo.xml` naming the series, episode, title, author, date and this
licence was added. The pages are otherwise as published.

MComix uses the book as a comic with real pages in its tests and in the
screenshots of its documentation.
